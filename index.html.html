I am Prashant Raj, a registered Advocate with the Bar Council of Uttar Pradesh and a holder of the Certificate of Practice (COP) under AIBE 19, authorising me to appear before courts across India.

Armed with an LLB (First Division, University of Lucknow) and an M.Com (IGNOU), I bring a unique blend of legal expertise and commercial understanding to every matter I handle — from bail applications in Sessions Court to complex M&A documentation and IBC proceedings before the NCLT.

Currently deepening my expertise through a Certificate Course in Corporate Law, M&A & Due Diligence at Lawctopus Law School, I am actively involved in drafting SPAs, Term Sheets, Due Diligence Reports, and IBC Section 7 Petitions — bridging theoretical rigor with real-world transactional practice.
